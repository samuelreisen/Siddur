    El Malei Rachamim

    Comforting Mourners

    הַמָּקוֹם יְנַחֵם אֶתְכֶם בְּתוֹךְ שְׁאָר אֲבֵלֵי צִיּוֹן וִירוּשָׁלָיִם

    “May the Omnipresent comfort you among the other mourners of Zion and Jerusalem.” To one man say otcha; to one woman, otach.
