    El Malei Rachamim

    Comforting mourners

    הַמָּקוֹם יְנַחֵם אֶתְכֶם בְּתוֹךְ שְׁאָר אֲבֵלֵי צִיּוֹן וִירוּשָׁלָיִם

    "May the Omnipresent comfort you among the other mourners of Zion and Jerusalem." Say otcha to one man, otach to one woman.
